## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful curation, quiet intellectual sanctuary, and tactile editorial grace. Designed for personal professional book archiving and reflective reading logs, the interface feels like an afternoon spent in an architect-designed private library: sunlit linen surfaces, smooth stone paper, natural botanical accents, and stillness.

### Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic combines **Editorial Warm Minimalism** with **Organic Tactility**. It prioritizes breath, natural materials, and literary dignity over high-frequency digital stimulation. Interfaces must never feel busy, cramped, or starkly monochromatic.

### Emotional Targets
- **Centering & Calm:** Replacing cognitive overload with deliberate white space and balanced proportions.
- **Intellectual Reverence:** Evoking fine typography, archival bookplates, and the tangible pleasure of reading.
- **Clarity & Permanence:** Clean structural grids paired with soft, reassuring organic hues.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in unbleached vellum paper, herbal sage leaves, and soft graphite ink.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **App Canvas (Paper Light):** `#FAF9F5` — Soft, warm linen parchment for the default viewport background.
- **Surface Level 1 (Warm Paper / Card Base):** `#F5F2EB` — Gentle, elevated card backgrounds providing subtle depth without harsh contrast.
- **Surface Level 2 (Subtle Recess / Pill Fills):** `#EDE8DE` — Used for inset containers, search inputs, and inactive filters.

### Accents (Botanical Sage)
- **Primary Accent (`#4A6B5D`):** Deep eucalyptus; drives primary actions, active book states, and foundational accents.
- **Secondary Accent (`#608072`):** Soft sage green; reserved for secondary interactive states, tag highlights, and hover nuances.
- **Muted Sage (`#DDE6E0`):** Ultra-soft tinted background for selected pills, subtle badges, and reading milestones.

### Neutrals & Borders
- **Primary Ink (`#2D312E`):** Soft botanical charcoal; delivers uncompromising readability without the jarring weight of true black.
- **Secondary Ink (`#5A605B`):** Muted stone charcoal; dedicated to metadata, timestamps, page counts, and publication credits.
- **Tertiary Ink (`#838B84`):** Whispering slate; placeholder text, inactive icons, and supplementary footnotes.
- **Structural Border (`#E6E2D8`):** Crisp, delicate stone border for cards and dividers.
- **Border Subtle (`#D8D2C5`):** Grounded taupe border for active inputs and delicate boundary definitions.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intimate editorial dialogue: **EB Garamond** imparts historical gravitas and literary warmth for titles, quotes, and author headings, while **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides pristine, open-aperture legibility for functional scanning, metadata, categorization, and study notes.

### Usage Directives
- **Serif Primacy:** Use EB Garamond in regular (400) and medium (500) weights. Allow italic styles for book subtitles, epigraphs, and personal annotations.
- **Sans-Serif Functional Rigor:** Reserve Plus Jakarta Sans for reading statistics, UI buttons, table headers, ISBNs, and tagging systems.
- **Spacious Rhythm:** Keep paragraph line-heights generous (minimum 1.6) to reduce visual fatigue during prolonged reading log sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an organic **12-column adaptive fluid grid** bounded by a maximum content container of `76rem` (1216px). Air and negative space are treated as active structural elements rather than empty voids.

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, 32px gutters, and 48px to 72px page margins. Book shelves display in a 4- or 5-item cadence or a wide two-column list with deep margin notes.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, and 32px page margins. Shelves adapt to a balanced 3-item grid.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, and 16px page margins. Content flows linearly; book details switch from split view to vertical stacking with floating bottom action anchors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal paper layering** and **low-contrast stone borders**, supplemented by soft ambient illumination. Harsh drop shadows are prohibited to maintain a serene, uncluttered aesthetic.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAF9F5`. The foundational desk surface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf Layer):** `#F5F2EB`, bounded by a `1px solid #E6E2D8` border. Rests flat on Level 0 or lifts softly on hover.
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets & Menus):** `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF9F5`, with a hairline border `#D8D2C5` and an ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 49, 46, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 49, 46, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Reader Overlays):** `#FAF9F5` combined with an ambient scrim of `rgba(45, 49, 46, 0.25)` softened with a subtle backdrop filter (`backdrop-blur: 4px`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses soft, natural geometries reminiscent of rounded book bindings and smoothed stones. 

- **Base Components (Cards, Inputs, Dialogue Boxes):** Formed with `0.5rem` (8px) to `1rem` (16px) corner radii.
- **Pills & Chips (Tags, Statuses, Quick-Filters):** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create an inviting, tactile contrast against rectilinear book cover art.
- **Book Cover Frames:** Bounded by a subtle `4px` radius with a `1px` inner rim highlight to echo genuine book jacket production.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep eucalyptus fill (`#4A6B5D`), text in `#FAF9F5`, pill-shaped or softly rounded (`0.75rem`), height `44px`. Hover shifts to `#3E5B4E` with a gentle transition (`200ms ease`).
- **Secondary / Outline Button:** Background in `transparent` or `#F5F2EB`, border `1px solid #D8D2C5`, text in `#2D312E`. Hover transitions to `#EDE8DE`.
- **Ghost Button:** Zero border, text in `#5A605B`. Hover reveals subtle `#E6E2D8` fill with `#2D312E` typography.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills (Reading, Finished, Wishlist):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `28px`, padding `0 12px`.
  - *Reading:* Fill `#DDE6E0`, border `1px solid #CAD7CF`, text `#3E5B4E`.
  - *Finished:* Fill `#EAE6DC`, border `1px solid #D8D2C5`, text `#5A605B`.
  - *Reference / Shelf Tags:* Fill `transparent`, border `1px solid #E6E2D8`, text `#5A605B`.

### Book Cards
- **Structure:** Surface Level 1 (`#F5F2EB`) with a `1px solid #E6E2D8` border and `16px` inner padding.
- **Cover Display:** 2:3 aspect ratio, subtle inner shadow to mimic paper spine spine depth (`inset -1px 0 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`).
- **Metadata Alignment:** Title set in EB Garamond Medium (`headline-sm`), author in Plus Jakarta Sans Regular (`body-sm`), progress and custom shelf tags nested at the bottom edge.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Height `44px`, background `#EDE8DE` (default) transitioning to `#FAF9F5` on focus.
- **Border:** `1px solid #E6E2D8` default; focus shifts to `1.5px solid #4A6B5D` with zero outward neon glow rings.
- **Typography:** Text input rendered in `#2D312E`, placeholder set in `#838B84`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection States:** `20px` bounds with rounded corners (`4px` for check, `50%` for radio). Inactive border `#D8D2C5` on `#F5F2EB`. Active state is filled with `#4A6B5D` featuring an unbleached `#FAF9F5` check or pip mark.

### Library-Specific Elements
- **Reading Progress Bar:** Track in `#E6E2D8` (`4px` height, rounded full), progress fill in `#608072`.
- **Annotation Quotes:** Left-aligned `2px` solid stroke in `#608072`, padding left `20px`, set in EB Garamond Italic (`20px`).
